# TenVoices: Speaker Consent Form

**Project:** TenVoices, speech recognition from ten voices (university course project, North South University, 2026)
**Team:** Tanvir Ahmed, Md. Shahriar Rakib Rabbi

## What we are doing
We are building and testing a computer system that turns speech into text. To check how well it works, we are recording 10 people reading short English sentences and answering a few simple questions.

## What you will do
- Read about 100 short sentences and voice commands aloud, and answer 5 simple questions (about 30 minutes).
- Read 10 of the sentences again in a noisier place (for example, a corridor or cafeteria).

## What we store
- Your voice recordings and their text transcripts.
- A speaker code (for example, S07). **Your name is never stored with the recordings.**
- Basic details you choose to give: gender, age band, home division, first language, the device used.

We will not ask for, and you should not say, your name, address, phone number or other personal details in the recordings.

## How the recordings are used
- To train and test speech recognition models for this project, and in the project report, poster and presentation.
- **Only if you tick "Yes" below:** published openly as part of the TenVoices dataset under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) licence, so other students and researchers can use them.

## Your rights
- Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time during the session.
- You can withdraw your recordings at any time **before the public release (planned for November 2026)** by contacting any team member. We will then delete them.
- Once the dataset is published, copies may already exist elsewhere and cannot be recalled.
