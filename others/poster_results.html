TAKEAWAYS

  • Self-supervised pre-training is what makes 50 commands learnable from 7 training speakers.
  • MFCC models depend heavily on the speaker: they drop on new voices and fall to chance in noise.
  • White-noise augmentation trades a little clean accuracy for robustness at 5–10 dB.
  • Zero-shot Whisper is the strongest and most portable recogniser, but accents raise its word errors.
