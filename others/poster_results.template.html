Takeaways

  • 1Self-supervised pre-training carries over to new voices.
  • 2MFCC models depend on the speaker and fail in noise.
  • 3Training with noise keeps wav2vec 2.0 usable at 5 dB.
  • 4Zero-shot Whisper is best; accents add word errors.
